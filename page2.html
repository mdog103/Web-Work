More Productivity Habits for Students

Ways to be prepare for an exam

  1. Start studying for an exam at least 2 weeks in advance.
  2. Gather all necessary materials and resurources prior to an exam.
  3. Create a study schedule that will work for you and is effective.

Best habits to have in college

  • DO not procrastinate on assignments. Work becomes more stressfull to the point of being unbearable
  • Have a healthy breakfast before an exam. anything to sugary can drain your energy that is needed for the exam.
  • If you feel anxious before an exam, try to take deep breaths and remind yourself that you are prepared.

Common bad habits and stronger strategies

Having good habits in college can lead to improved academic performance, better time management, and reduced stress levels. Some common habits that can prevent success could be rereading or studying only the topic that feels easy to you.There are plenty of weak sections of learning that need improvement. Even if it means getting out of your comfort zone, it's important to challenge yourself and focus on areas that need work. So starting with the hardest section gradually can be a good way. Watching videos also might not work if it is different than what the professor teaches. Try your best to align your study methods and learn the approach that works best for you.

It is best to seek feedback from professors as early as possible before the time time the exam is scheduled. You can ask for clarification on any concepts you are unsure about. As long as you are willing to put in effort and show enough care Faliure can be an impossibility. There are many resources avaliable to help you succeed. there are study groups and tutoring services avaliable. As much as productivity is important, it is also important to maintain sleep, excercise,downtime, and healthy eating throughout your college life.
