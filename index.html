Productivity Habits for Students

      Ways to be productive during the weekday

      1. prioritize tasks from the most important first to the least important last
      2. excercise often for at least 30 minutes a day. This improves your focus and energy
      3. If an assignment or task seems to intimidating, work on it 15 minutes then take a break the small amount of work you do will build motivation in continuing the task.
      4. get enough sleep each night which is at least 7-8 hours per day.

      Ways to be productive toward your mental health improvement during the weekend

      • Take breaks regulary to avoid burnout which can occur if you overdo assignments or etc
      • Get into your favorite activities or hobbies such as reading, listening to music, or spending time with your friends and family.
      • Near the end of the week have goals you want to acheive by the end of next week.

      The importance of productivity in college

      .

      One of the important skills for students to devlop in college is time Managment. Starting long-term assignments early were you can break up the assignment into smaller, manageable tasks. To make studying less overwhelming you could study for 25 minutes then take a 5 minute break each. According to lynn University this is known as the pomodoro tecnique. Also it is important to get into a daily routine for example every day from 4pm to 6pm you study for an upcoming exam you have in advance. What matters in that is consistency for each of the courses you have.

      Another important skill to have in the importance of productivity in college is also finding ways to contribute to your learning experience in college. What you could do is getting a job in some places on campus. You can also go to charity races as well if advertised. Tutoring could even be an idea to help students who have less experience on subjects. There is also finding a connecting group or oganization to be a part of in which you can engage and build relationships. If their is a church on campus you can find ways to connect spiritually along with other members who do the same.
